Reverse wood is an eco-friendly, easily shaped material. We used it for a table with integrated mirrors, symbolizing how identity reflects its surroundings, and for a lamp with a built-in mirror that projects stencil-shaped shadows, reflecting an identity shaped by personal and environmental influences.

Empheral Reflection, a short film made in Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, explores identity through the two Reverse Wood works and a spontaneous, interview-based social experiment. It reveals how people perceive themselves and how the designs reflect the layered nature of identity.
