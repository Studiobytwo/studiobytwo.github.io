Off the Charts is Statista's regular talk show about the biggest trends shaping business, technology, society and consumer behaviour, with a rotating panel of Statista experts.

Together with a few others, we worked on the series from start to finish: set and lighting design, help with the scripts, camera and sound, editing, colour grading, and the typography of the episodes and their short clips.
