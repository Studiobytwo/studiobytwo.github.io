Ask Tiago is the Instagram channel of Tiago Bianchi, who covers AI and digital trends at Statista. His short reels turn data into a quick take, from bot traffic and the Dead Internet Theory to marketing and AI.

Most of the reels are our work: set and lighting, help with the scripts, camera and sound, editing, colour grading and typography.
