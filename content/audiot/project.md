A music-inspired website design for a streaming app, made in Adobe XD and Illustrator. A custom logo and wave elements represent sound and rhythm, and the colour palette was chosen to match the theme.

The logo is built from simple geometric shapes for clarity and balance. It is inspired by a speaker, using circles and a triangle. Overlapping elements were removed for a cleaner form.
